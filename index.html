Questions, answered

Do my guests need to download anything?

No. The invitation is a link that opens in any phone's browser, on Android and iPhone, straight from WhatsApp.

Can I see a design before ordering?

Yes. Every design has a live demo: tap Live demo under it to open the real animated invitation with sample names.

What do I need to send?

Names, the date and venue. Everything else is optional: functions, your story, families, photos, dress code, UPI for gifts. The order form asks for it step by step.

The time isn't fixed yet. Can I still order?

Yes. Tick Time not decided yet and the invitation shows only the date.

Can every guest get their own link?

Yes. Send a list of names and each guest gets a link that greets them by name.

Can I get a video for my WhatsApp status?

Yes. Pick one of the 200 looks under Story videos and tap Order this video: we make it with your names, functions, date and venue. We can also send a video of your invitation card itself.

Ready for yours?

Wedding, kitty party, birthday or puja: message us and we'll design an invitation your guests will talk about.
